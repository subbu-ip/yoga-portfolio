About Sarah

Your personal guide to flexible, pain-free movement

I'm Subrahmanya, a dedicated yoga trainer from Bengaluru, specializing in backbending, flexibility, and mobility-focused flows for all levels.

My teaching blends traditional Hatha & Ashtanga principles with modern mobility work, helping students build strong, open, and resilient bodies without forcing or pain.

  • Certified yoga instructor with experience in group & 1:1 coaching.
  • Special focus on spine health, hip opening, and shoulder mobility.
  • Customized programs for beginners, athletes, and desk workers.
